Brand button for Datacenter Capitalinas — sky-blue primary fill, sky-lit outline and bare ghost variants on the dark ground.

```jsx
<Button variant="primary" size="lg">Conocer servicios</Button>
<Button variant="secondary">Contacto</Button>
<Button variant="ghost" size="sm">Ver más</Button>
```

Variants: `primary` (sky fill, glows on hover) · `secondary` (outline, brightens to sky on hover) · `ghost` (text only) · `on-light` (black fill for white surfaces). Sizes `sm | md | lg`. Supports `iconLeft`/`iconRight`, `fullWidth`, `disabled`. Label tracking is wide (0.04em) to echo the engraved wordmark.
